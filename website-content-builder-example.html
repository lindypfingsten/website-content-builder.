Website Content Builder

I use my content builder in ChatGPT to turn an idea into a page plan. This work sample illustrates how I would organize a page that introduces leaders to the BRITER™ Dashboard Builder.

The page brief

Audience
A leader who wants to connect what their organization measures to what it exists to do.
Reader’s question
How do I decide what to measure and who should own it?
Place on the website
Assessments → Design Your Dashboard. Readers can find it alongside tools they can use.
Next step
Open the dashboard, enter one strategic goal, and choose the first measure to track.

The page plan

  1. Start with the reader’s goal. Explain how the tool helps them connect mission to a practical decision.
  2. Show one example. Use a community center that wants new members to feel welcome and stay engaged.
  3. Make the next step clear. Link directly to the dashboard so the reader can try one goal with their own team.

What the reader sees

Design your dashboard

Start with one thing you want people to experience because of your work. If you want new members to feel welcome, track 90-day retention alongside what members say about belonging. Then ask staff where onboarding gets stuck.

In the builder, enter your goal, where you are now, what you want to see change, the evidence you’ll use, and who will follow up.
